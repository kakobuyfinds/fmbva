03 / Kakobuy spreadsheet guide

From a saved link
to a useful shortlist.

A good shopping list should help you answer a few practical questions: What is the item? Will it fit? What evidence is available? What might the total cost be?

Record what you can verify and mark missing information before moving forward.

  1. Start with one category

    Choose shoes, clothing, headwear, or accessories. Narrow the search by intended use, preferred fit, and budget.

  2. Record the exact listing

    Save the product URL, seller, selected variant, listed price, and the date you checked it. Similar photos can appear across different listings.

  3. Compare measurements and photos

    Use garment measurements or foot-length guidance where available. Review QC photos for the selected item and variant when those photos exist.

  4. Estimate the full cost

    Consider the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current charges before paying.

  5. Recheck before checkout

    Confirm stock, return terms, shipping restrictions, and whether your chosen purchasing service currently accepts the product link.

04 / Product comparison notes

Keep the useful fields.

Use these fields when building your own Kakobuy product spreadsheet or comparing finds on CAFANS.

A practical checklist for evaluating product links before making a purchase.
Field What to record Why it helps
Product & variant Item name, category, color, size, and seller URL. Keeps comparisons tied to the same version of an item.
Price & currency Listed price, currency, quantity, and date checked. Separates the product price from a complete delivered-cost estimate.
Measurements Foot length, garment dimensions, hat circumference, or accessory dimensions. Provides a better fit reference than a size label alone.
Photo evidence Listing images and available QC photos matched to the variant. Helps compare visible details without treating photos as authentication.
Materials & care Seller-disclosed composition, construction, and care instructions. Makes unsupported material claims easier to identify.
Shipping & terms Estimated packed weight, shipping quote, return policy, and restrictions. Highlights costs and conditions that may affect the buying decision.

How do I use a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

Choose a category, open a product listing, record the exact variant, compare measurements, and review available photos. Check current costs and purchasing-service compatibility before ordering. This page provides a guide and category links; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or place orders.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide linking to CAFANS. It does not represent Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès, and no partnership or endorsement is claimed.

Does a spreadsheet link guarantee a genuine product?

No. A spreadsheet entry, a brand name, a seller description, or a product photo does not establish authenticity. If authenticity matters to your purchase, use an authorized retailer and seek verifiable evidence.

What should I check in QC photos?

Look at the selected color and size, visible construction, stitching, alignment, surface condition, and any requested measurements. QC photos can help inspect visible details, but they do not prove authenticity or reveal every possible defect.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item that fits you well. For tops, check chest width, length, shoulders, and sleeves. For pants, compare waist, rise, inseam, and leg opening. Confirm whether measurements refer to the garment or the body.

Are product prices and shipping costs fixed?

No. Prices, stock, exchange rates, service fees, and shipping quotes may change. International delivery costs can depend on the destination, service, parcel dimensions, and chargeable weight. Obtain a current quote before paying.
